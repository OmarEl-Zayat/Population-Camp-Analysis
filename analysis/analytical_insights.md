# Analytical Insights — Displaced Population Dashboard

These findings were produced from the interactive Excel dashboard (PivotTables + DAX measures built on the cleaned dataset). They correspond to the Step 6 "Final Validation & Analysis" stage of the project.

## Validation

- All 9,917 rows loaded successfully with no null values remaining in the housing columns (Room, Flat, House, Tent).
- Gender percentages, ratios, and the average-people-per-house calculation are consistent — no division errors — and the average of 49.68 people per house matches the dashboard.

## Analysis Questions & Answers

**Which country has the highest total population?**
Yemen, with 23,960,127 people.

**Which country has the highest female percentage?**
Libya, at about 56.9%.

**What is the dominant housing type in each country?**
Flats are the most common housing type in Yemen, Libya, and Iraq. Syria is the exception, where tents are the dominant housing type.

**How does total population change across years?**
Total population declined slightly, from around 30.1 million in 2017 to 28.9 million in 2018.

**Which cities have the largest population?**
Ibb, Aden, Khantuman, Al Hudaydah, and Atma are the top five cities by population.

**What is the average number of people per house?**
49.68 people per house.

## Key Interpretations

- Most of the displaced population is concentrated in Yemen and Syria, indicating these two countries require the greatest share of humanitarian resources.
- Housing conditions differ sharply by country: most countries rely predominantly on flats, but Syria relies mostly on tents — pointing to less stable housing infrastructure there.
- Females make up a large majority of the population (roughly 56–57%) across all four countries.
- An average of nearly 50 people per house indicates severe overcrowding.
- The slight population decline between 2017 and 2018 may suggest that some displaced people were beginning to return home.
